What this does — and doesn't

Generates a simple pet-parent reminder covering arrival time, drop-off, coat condition, health/vaccination requirements as set by the salon, and pickup expectations. It does not verify vaccination status, collect payment, book appointments, send SMS, or replace professional judgment.
